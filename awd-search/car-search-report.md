## Read the verification level correctly

Every ranked vehicle below has specific advertised AWD evidence and an eligible transmission architecture. FWD vehicles, Nissan and conventional pulley/chain CVTs are excluded. A marketplace “CVT” label on the Toyota hybrids and the Honda two-motor hybrid does **not** mean a conventional belt CVT.

**Working AWD, actual mechanical condition and remaining battery life cannot be verified remotely.** These are conditional inspection candidates, not certified functioning vehicles. If your hard requirement means proof the rear axle currently works, no vehicle can receive an unconditional BUY until an independent technician tests it. Public listing visibility also does not establish physical stock. No dealer was contacted, no form submitted and no funds committed.

This report applies to the AWD/4WD-only requirements. The separate [general-search report](../general-search/car-search-report.md) includes FWD candidates, which are ineligible for this AWD mission.
