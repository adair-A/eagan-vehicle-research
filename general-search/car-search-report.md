## Research basis and boundaries

Functional CamoFox 2.4.8 was reused; no reinstall was necessary. Both required platforms were actively browsed using dedicated sessions, filters, individual pages and multiple result pages. Visor research saved **196 distinct VIN discoveries**; CarGurus saved **78 distinct listing IDs** across Lexus, Toyota pages 1–2 and Honda searches. These overlap and must not be added together as unique vehicles. National home-delivery entries appeared despite radius filters and were not selected as local finalists. Dealer sites and readable history reports supplemented marketplaces. Full pool and raw evidence are in the folder.

Important limitations: some dealer sites presented human-verification/CAPTCHA, and some CARFAX reports rendered only an iframe. No challenge was bypassed. Public listing activity is not physical availability. We visually reviewed exterior page photos for the top three but did not assess every gallery image or any underbody. Many full histories, records, VIN recalls, warranty eligibility and fees require seller documents. Search is broad and useful, not proof every available car in the region was captured. Scores remain conditional when evidence is missing.

Further sources: [reliability research](reliability-research.md), [Mazda supplement](bedrock-mazda3-supplement.md), [hybrid finalist verification](supplemental-finalist-verification.md), [comparison](vehicle-comparison.md), [search log](search-log.md). Minnesota [motor-vehicle sales tax](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/guide/motor-vehicle-sales) is 6.875% of taxable selling price; ordinary local sales tax is excluded, but applicable vehicle excise/registration/filing/plates/wheelage can add costs. [Typical transfer-fee guide](https://assets.dps.mn.gov/files/dvs/BuyingorSellingMotorVehicle_Just_the_Facts.pdf) supports the $36 baseline used here. A $15,000 advertised car exceeds $16,031 before title/registration. Request quotes before budgeting to the last dollar.
