## Prius c exact-powertrain and reliability supplement

Toyota's [2014 Prius c engineering release](https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-2014-prius-c-high-city-fuel-economy/) identifies the 1.5L Atkinson hybrid, motor/generator transaxle and 144V NiMH battery. Toyota's [Prius c release PDF search result](https://pressroom.toyota.com/?generate_pdf=28216) expressly identifies a planetary-type continuously variable transmission. Full Toyota newsroom fetch returned 403, but search indexing exposed the manufacturer's description. Its [2016 pocket source book, manufacturer document hosted by Toyota dealer](https://pictures.dealer.com/antiochtoyotascion/7b52f2830a0e0acc0800654572128a02.pdf) confirms 1.5L, 99 system HP and **ECVT**, 50 combined MPG. This is an eligible hybrid power-split eCVT. Prius c is not the 1.8L Prius liftback/CT/Prius v engine; do not indiscriminately apply their oil-consumption or head-gasket bulletin scope to it.

However, it has documented brake-system risk. Toyota [21TE01](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2021/MC-10198140-9999.pdf) covers certain 2012–2014 Prius c booster/pump internal malfunctions. Verification codes include C1391/C1252/C1253/C1256. Original secondary coverage was 10 years/150,000 miles, normally time-expired on a 2014 by now. Toyota [24TE01 dealer letter](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2024/MC-10252267-9999.pdf) and [FAQ](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2024/MC-11011632-0001.pdf) cover certain 2015–2017 Prius c. Primary period ended March 31, 2025; secondary period is 10 years/150,000 miles. A 2016's exact in-service date matters in October 2026. These are condition-based support programs, not a statement every car fails or all brake work is free. Verify VIN/coverage and repaired-part invoices; scan the ABS ECU and assess accumulator/pump operation during PPI.

Battery aging, cooling intake cleanliness, suspension/rust and accident repair quality remain relevant on both cars. The local rebuilt battery supplier cited in reliability-research.md lists Prius c at $850 including tax/install with core and 12-month warranty; a rebuilt pack is not equivalent to a new OEM pack, nor does that price establish installed condition or future reliability.
